4 August 2026 · Aberfoyle LiDAR dissertation · council framework adapted from the user-provided LLM Council skill

Council verdict

The centroid problem is real but not the largest threat. The first priority is to separate three different tasks—contemporaneous height estimation, genuine future forecasting, and retrospective environmental attribution—and enforce prediction-time and fold-provenance rules for each. Freeze model architectures; correct confirmed data defects; rerun only affected fixed models; reserve a fresh or nested spatial evaluation for final confirmatory claims.

Recommended sequence

  1. Approve a feature/target provenance table defining the estimand and prediction-time availability.
  2. Correct survey-time Thin and invalid centroid points; fix misleading documentation.
  3. Regenerate affected features and rerun frozen architectures/hyperparameters on identical splits/seeds.
  4. Run acquisition-year QC and clustered/spatial robustness checks.
  5. Use a fresh locked spatial holdout or nested spatial CV for final headline performance.
