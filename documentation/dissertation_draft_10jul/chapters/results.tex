\subsection*{Spatial map of growth ceilings (planned)}

The main deliverable of this dissertation is a spatial map of Aberfoyle showing the learned growth ceiling $\hat{y}_{\max}(\mathbf{e})$ for each plot. Figure~\ref{fig:spatial-map-example} shows the kind of multi-panel spatial map planned, here coloured by observed top height in each survey year rather than by model output. The final version will replace this year-by-year layout with a model-by-model layout: one panel each for the CR baseline, XGB-B predicted residuals, the baseline PINN, and the Env-PINN. I plan to build this final figure directly on top of the map shown here, adding the Env-PINN growth-ceiling layer once that model is trained, so the spatial pattern of terrain-driven growth limits becomes visible alongside the raw height data already mapped.

\begin{figure}[h]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figures/legacy_examples/spatial_map_by_year_example.png}
\caption{Example figure from an earlier Aberfoyle dataset/workflow. Included to illustrate the planned analysis; not a final result. Observed top height across Aberfoyle by LiDAR survey year, from an earlier exploratory pass on a prior GeoPackage version. The final dissertation figure will use this same map layout, but with one panel per model (CR, XGBoost, baseline PINN, Env-PINN) instead of one panel per year, so model outputs can be compared spatially. The Env-PINN panel, showing the learned growth ceiling $\hat{y}_{\max}(\mathbf{e})$, will be added once that model is trained.}
\label{fig:spatial-map-example}
\end{figure}

\section{Synthesis (placeholder)}

Once the results above exist, this section will report: the share of between-plot growth variation that terrain and wind explain (from the XGB-B $R^2$); whether Moran's I on the XGBoost residuals drops relative to Moran's I on the raw CR residuals, which would suggest the features capture real spatial structure rather than mere proximity; and what share of variation stays unexplained, expected to include a contribution from unrecorded thinning (Chapter~\ref{ch:data}).
