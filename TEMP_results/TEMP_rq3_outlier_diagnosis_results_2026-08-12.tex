### Headline finding — these are not unexplained environmental signal, they're almost certainly data-quality/disturbance artifacts

**Every one of the 5 worst plots has an observed `local_y_max_difference` beyond 50 in magnitude**
— against this target's overall mean of 3.94 and SD of 10.49 (RQ3 EN/XGBoost note), these sit
**5+ standard deviations from the mean**. No smooth environmental gradient plausibly explains a
single-plot deviation of this size from the shared curve.

**4 of 5 are spatially clustered** — x/y within a few km of each other, compartments 2232, 2217,
2173, 2176 all adjacent (2100s-2200s range), only compartment 1135 separate. Clustered extreme
errors, not independent random noise, strongly suggest a shared cause — a disturbance event, a
measurement-year data issue, or a shared extraction problem for that specific area.

**Every one of the 5 shows a large `chelsa_bio12_precip_mm` SHAP push (+0.82 to +3.41) and the
model still can't get anywhere near the observed value** (predictions all land at 2.8-6.0, nowhere
close to 50-60) — this is not the model "missing the right variable," it's the model hitting a
real ceiling. No environmental variable in Set4 is capable of explaining a deviation this
extreme, which is exactly what you'd expect if these plots are disturbance/reset/data-quality
cases rather than genuine environmental attribution targets.
