### Headline finding

**The win is real, not a lucky single seed.** 4survey's R2 varies by only ±0.0035 across 5
independent training runs (min 0.654, max 0.663) — remarkably tight, well inside the original
seed 42's own 95% CI width (~0.065). 6survey is somewhat less stable (SD 0.0078, seed 46 an
outlier on the low side at 0.688) but every one of the 5 seeds still lands within or very close to
seed 42's own CI — nothing here overturns the "statistically tied with PINN, not beaten" read
from `TEMP_rq1_sweep_results_2026-08-11.tex`.

**6survey's bias is consistently negative across all 5 seeds** (-0.038 to -0.259, never positive)
— DNN systematically underpredicts height on 6survey regardless of training seed, a real,
seed-independent pattern, not noise. 4survey's bias has no consistent direction across seeds
(ranges from -0.070 to +0.144, crossing zero) — no systematic bias concern there.
