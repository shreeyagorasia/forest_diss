### How these numbers were generated

- **Fit** (cluster, GPU): 3 models (`dnn_env_terrain`/`pinn_env_terrain`/`pinn_env_terrain_k`) x
  3 sets x 2 cohorts x 5 folds (`spatial_block_kfold`, split seed 42) = 90 fits. Single seed —
  this is the comparison sweep to pick a winner, not the final robustness-checked number (that's
  Step 4's 5-seed reseed of the winner only, not yet run).
- **Evaluate** (local): `models.common.kfold_summary` per run_name — pools all 5 folds' held-out
  predictions into ONE population-level R2/RMSE/MAE/bias (not a naive average of 5 separate R2s),
  computes a cluster-bootstrap 95% CI on the pooled R2 (resampling whole compartments, not
  individual plots, since plots within a compartment aren't independent), and separately reports
  the per-fold R2 mean/SD (the "if you'd only used one fold" precision comparison).
- `pinn_env_terrain_k`'s evaluate additionally reports a pooled Spearman correlation between its
  two learned per-plot parameters (`learned_y_max`, `learned_k`) — a model-identifiability check
  (see `models/pinn_env_terrain_k/check_cr_identifiability.py`'s own header for why this is
  tracked), not a predictive-accuracy metric.

### Headline pattern

**DNN wins on 4survey, PINN variants win on 6survey — not a universal winner.** On 4survey, DNN
beats every PINN variant by a wide margin (0.637–0.659 vs. 0.577–0.579 pooled R2) — consistent
with this project's own earlier baseline finding (`TEMP_baseline_env_new_sets_results_2026-08-10.tex`)
that simple RF/XGBoost baselines also beat PINN-with-terrain on 4survey specifically. On 6survey,
the pattern flips: PINN variants (0.705–0.710) edge out DNN (0.650–0.707), though DNN's Set3
number (0.707) is close to PINN's best. **Set3 is the best- or near-best set for every model on
6survey**; on 4survey, DNN peaks at Set3 too, while PINN's three sets are statistically
indistinguishable from each other (all three 95% CIs overlap almost completely: 0.545–0.608
range for all of Set2/3/4). **Set4 does not help, and sometimes hurts** (DNN's worst 4survey and
6survey numbers are both at Set4) — consistent with the project's standing "widening isn't
guaranteed to help" finding.

**6survey's CIs are much wider than 4survey's** (e.g. DNN Set2: 6survey CI width 0.158 vs.
4survey CI width 0.065) — expected, given 6survey's bootstrap resamples only 47 compartments vs.
4survey's 232, so any 6survey-only claim should be read with real caution.
