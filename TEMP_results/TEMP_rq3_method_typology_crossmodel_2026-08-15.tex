## TEMP method note — RQ3 outlier typology: cross-model agreement + sub-compartment clustering (2026-08-15)

**Applies to**: RQ3 only.

**What this evaluates**: are the 266 flagged plots (from the broader Tukey-fence scan) hard for
EN/GNNWR too, or just XGBoost? And do flagged plots cluster spatially within a compartment (points
toward a localized event) or spread evenly through it (points toward a whole-compartment lookup
error)?

**How** (pseudocode):
1. Cross-model agreement: load EN/XGBoost residuals from the shared `predictions.csv`; load GNNWR
   by concatenating its 5 seed-42 fold `test_predictions.csv` files and using `denormalized_pred_result`
   as the prediction column. For each model, rank all plots by `|residual|` (`.rank(pct=True)`), then
   check what fraction of the 266 flagged plots fall in each model's own worst 1%/5%.
2. Sub-compartment clustering: for each of the 6 most-flagged compartments, compute each flagged
   plot's distance to the CENTROID of the flagged plots in that compartment (using `x`/`y`), and
   compare the mean of that to the same quantity computed over ALL plots in that compartment (their
   distance to the whole-compartment centroid). Flagged-subset distance smaller than whole-
   compartment distance = spatially clustered, not spread evenly.

**Full results**: `TEMP_rq3_outlier_diagnosis_results_2026-08-12.tex`'s "Outlier typology:
cross-model agreement, sub-compartment clustering" section — ~36x over-representation in each
model's worst-1% bucket across all 3 models; flagged plots consistently closer together than their
compartment as a whole, in every compartment checked.
